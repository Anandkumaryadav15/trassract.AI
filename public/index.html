Frequently Asked Questions

Do students require any prior coding or electronics experience? +
No prior experience is necessary! Our curriculum is carefully tiered. Younger learners start with block coding (Scratch Jr.) and snap circuits before progressing to Python, C++, and hardware schematics.
Can a school operate the lab without hiring full-time AI experts? +
Absolutely. We train your existing science and computer teachers with our structured pedagogical modules, provide detailed lesson plans, and dispatch our master mentors for regular support.
Are the kits included for online courses? +
Yes! When you enroll in any of our online live courses, a complete hardware kit containing all components, boards, and sensors is delivered straight to your doorstep before the first class.
Can we book a free demonstration session? +
Yes! You can fill out the enquiry form below or click the WhatsApp button to book a personalized 1-on-1 demo session with our lead robotics educators.
